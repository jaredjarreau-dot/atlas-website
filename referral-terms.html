Client Referral Program Terms

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version
  • Introduce a new organization through the referral form before it signs an agreement with Atlas.
  • If it signs within 6 months and Atlas collects at least $5,000 in professional fees, you earn 10% of the professional fees collected in the first 6 months: at least $500, at most $3,000.
  • You can take the reward as a payment or as a donation to a nonprofit you choose. If you work at the organization you refer, the reward is a donation.
  • Atlas pays within 30 days after the reward is final. The full terms below control.

1. The program

Atlas Strategies Consulting LLC ("Atlas") offers this Client Referral Program (the "Program") to thank people who introduce new clients. By submitting a referral or accepting a reward, you (the "Referrer") agree to these terms. Atlas may update, suspend or end the Program at any time. Referrals submitted before a change are handled under the terms in effect when they were submitted.

2. Definitions

  • Referred Client: the organization you introduce to Atlas.
  • Qualifying Engagement: a written agreement between Atlas and the Referred Client for consulting services with professional fees of at least $5,000.
  • Professional Fees: fees Atlas charges for consulting services. Professional Fees do not include pass-through or reimbursed costs (such as survey data, software or licenses), travel and other expenses, or taxes.
  • Reward Period: the 6 months beginning on the date Atlas receives the Referred Client's first payment.
  • Reward: the referral fee or donation described in Section 5.

3. Who can refer

To earn a Reward, you must be an individual (not a company), at least 18 years old and a legal resident of the United States. You cannot participate if you are an employee, owner, contractor or subcontract partner of Atlas, or an immediate family or household member of one. You also cannot earn a Reward for referring an organization you own, or one where you are the person who decides whether to hire Atlas.

4. What makes a referral qualify

  • Submitted first, through the website. You must submit the referral through the referral form on atlas-strategies.co before the Referred Client signs an agreement with Atlas. Referrals made after an agreement is signed do not qualify.
  • Consent and complete information. Before you submit, the contact must agree to be introduced to and contacted by Atlas. The form must include the contact's name, organization, email and a description of their needs.
  • A new client. The Referred Client cannot be a current or former Atlas client, or an organization Atlas has had proposal or sales discussions with in the previous 12 months.
  • Signed within 6 months. The Referred Client must sign a Qualifying Engagement within 6 months of your submission. Atlas may, at its sole discretion, honor a referral that signs later.
  • First referral counts. If more than one person refers the same organization, the first complete submission through the website qualifies.

5. The Reward

  • Amount. 10% of the Professional Fees Atlas collects from the Referred Client during the Reward Period, with a minimum of $500 and a maximum of $3,000 per Referred Client. A Reward is earned only if Atlas collects at least $5,000 in Professional Fees from the Referred Client during the Reward Period.
  • Based on payments received. The Reward is calculated only on fees Atlas has actually received, not on fees invoiced or quoted.
  • When it's paid. Atlas pays the Reward within 30 days after the earliest of: (a) the end of the Reward Period, (b) the end of the engagement, or (c) the date the Reward reaches $3,000.
  • Donation option. You may choose to have the Reward donated to a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit you name instead of paid to you. If you are employed by the Referred Client or one of its affiliates, the Reward is paid only as a donation. Donations are made by Atlas, and you do not receive the payment or any tax benefit from it.
  • How it's paid. Referral fees are paid by check, mailed to the address on your IRS Form W-9.

6. Exclusions

No Reward is paid for:

  • Government and public-sector organizations, or any work awarded through a public procurement, RFP or bid process.
  • Work Atlas performs as a subcontractor or through a partner firm.
  • Speaking, training, workshops, products or other work that is not a consulting engagement.
  • A Referred Client whose work you are already being paid for by Atlas under a separate agreement (for example, as a subcontractor or partner on that engagement).
  • Referrals submitted without the contact's consent, or in bulk (for example, from a purchased or scraped list).

Repeat referrers are welcome: each new client you refer can earn its own Reward.

A referral does not obligate Atlas to accept an engagement. Atlas may decline any engagement for any reason, including conflicts with existing clients.

7. Taxes

Before a referral fee is paid, you must provide a completed and signed IRS Form W-9 (page 1 only; the remaining pages are instructions). Atlas will tell you how to send it securely. Atlas will issue IRS Form 1099-NEC when required by law. You are responsible for any taxes on a referral fee. If you do not provide a valid taxpayer identification number, Atlas may hold the payment or withhold amounts required by law.

8. Your obligations

  • Your employer's policies. You are responsible for making sure that participating and accepting a Reward is allowed by your employer's policies and any agreements you have. If your employer does not allow you to accept a payment, the Reward will be made as a donation.
  • Professional rules. If you are subject to professional, licensing or fiduciary rules (for example, as a benefits broker, insurance producer, accountant or attorney), you are responsible for complying with them, including any disclosure to your client. If those rules do not permit a payment, the Reward will be made as a donation.
  • No improper payments. You will comply with all applicable anti-bribery, anti-kickback and anti-corruption laws, and will not offer or accept anything of value to influence a hiring decision.
  • Independent relationship. The Program does not make you an employee, agent or partner of Atlas. You may not make commitments or statements on Atlas's behalf.
  • Confidentiality and privacy. Keep confidential any non-public information about Atlas or the Referred Client you learn through the Program, and follow applicable privacy and anti-spam laws when sharing a contact's information.

9. Atlas's decisions

Atlas decides in its reasonable discretion whether a referral qualifies and how much is owed, and may request information to verify a referral. Atlas may withhold a Reward in cases of fraud, abuse or a violation of these terms. The Reward is calculated on Professional Fees Atlas keeps, after any refunds or credits issued before the Reward is paid. Once paid, a Reward will not be reclaimed, except in cases of fraud.

10. Limitation of liability

The Program is provided as is. To the extent permitted by law, Atlas is not liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages related to the Program, and Atlas's total liability to any Referrer is limited to the amount of the Reward owed for that referral.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts serving Chesterfield County, Virginia.
