Client Referral Program

The best introductions come from people who hear the frustration first: the plant manager who can't explain why two people in the same job are paid differently, the HR leader staring down a pay transparency law, the CEO whose bonus plan pays out without moving the numbers. If someone in your network is wrestling with pay, introduce us. If they become a client, you're rewarded for it.

How it works

Step 1

Ask your contact if they're open to an introduction.

Step 2

Submit the referral below, before they sign an agreement.

Step 3

Once they're a client and I've been paid, you collect your reward.

Your reward: 10% of the professional fees Atlas collects from the new client in their first 6 months, with a minimum of $500 and a maximum of $3,000.

Prefer to give it away? Choose a donation to a nonprofit of your choice instead.

Engagements must be at least $5,000 in professional fees. If you work at the organization you're referring, the reward is a donation. Full details are in the program terms.

You'll know it's a fit when you hear…

  • “Our pay structure grew one exception at a time, and nobody can explain it anymore.”
  • “We have more job titles than jobs, and the job descriptions don't match the work.”
  • “Pay transparency is coming, and we're not ready to post our ranges.”
  • “I'm worried about what a pay equity review would find.”
  • “Our bonus plan pays out, but it doesn't change results.”
  • “AI is changing our roles, and we don't know what skills or pay they need now.”
